Last Call: Spirits on the Rocks

Simulation | Survival
Game Intro: https://youtu.be/b2_PPrGrUPY

Overview

A first-person horror bartending game set in Satan’s bar. A cruel alcoholic is condemned to serve monstrous patrons after death, seeking redemption through punishment. Players read orders, collect ingredients, mix cocktails, and serve drinks before time runs out. Intoxicated customers can become violent, forcing players to flee upstairs or hide behind a fragile door. Distinct monster abilities, including slowing slime and a freezing gaze, turn ordinary bar service into a struggle for survival.

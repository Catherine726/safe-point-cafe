PROJECT DOSSIER / 05

One Last Dance

 Rhythm | Survival 
 Available to play at: https://k1rito5.itch.io/one-last-dance

In this game, you will play as a bird with a lifespan of only 20 seconds, along with its offspring, experiencing the reproduction and civilizational development of birds.

The game has four endings. Interact with special birds in each generation, and unlock an ending after reaching a certain number of interactions, or when your eggs reach 50, a special ending will also be unlocked!

Life is short -- so the primary task is to encounter more partners, and leave as many offspring as possible! Good Luck!
